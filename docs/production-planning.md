# Case Study 03 · Production Planning and Execution

**Environment:** Academic SAP manufacturing exercise, simulated training-company data.

## Business scenario

Prepare master data, plan manufacturing requirements, and trace a production order through component consumption, receipt of finished goods, and order settlement.

## Exercise milestones

1. **Material master data:** recorded completion of three finished-product master records.
2. **BOM and routing:** assigned **10 bill-of-material components** to routing activities.
3. **Product group:** assigned planned product mix proportions of **40% / 30% / 30%**.
4. **Requirements planning:** maintained planned independent requirements of **100 units per month** for 12 periods for each of three training products.
5. **Production order:** recorded a **100-unit** manufacturing order.
6. **Initial component inventory:** posted **500 units** for each of 10 training components.
7. **Goods issue:** recorded component consumption for the production process.
8. **Confirmation:** recorded production completion with a **100-unit yield**.
9. **Goods receipt:** documented the finished-goods receiving event.
10. **Order settlement:** the exercise report recorded completion without a remaining balance.

## What this demonstrates

- Familiarity with the relationship between planning and execution.
- Understanding of BOMs, routing operations, demand planning, production orders, and material movement.
- Ability to trace a multi-stage operational process and describe the impact of each step.
- Practice interpreting status messages and documentation in enterprise software.
